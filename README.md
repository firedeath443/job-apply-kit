# Job apply kit

Your own job-application assistant, running on your own Claude and your own computer. Every day it:

1. searches Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, remote job boards, government jobs and company career sites for the jobs you want;
2. ranks them against your experience, pay and location;
3. writes a resume tailored to each job, using only things you've confirmed are true;
4. fills out the application in your Chrome, and submits it or waits for your OK, whichever you choose;
5. watches your Gmail for interviews and shows everything on a private dashboard you can open on your phone.

Nothing goes to anyone else. Your data stays in the `me/` folder on your computer and in your own Claude account.

## What you need
- A paid Claude plan. Each application uses a lot of usage, so bigger plans do more per day.
- A Windows or Mac computer that's on, with Chrome open, when the run happens.
- The Claude desktop app and the Claude in Chrome extension.

## Get started
1. Install the [Claude desktop app](https://claude.ai/download) and open its **Code** tab.
2. Start a new session in any folder, and paste this:

> Download https://github.com/firedeath443/job-apply-kit into a folder called job-apply-kit in my Documents, switch into that folder, then read CLAUDE.md and set me up.

Claude walks you through the rest in about 15 minutes: your resume or LinkedIn, what jobs you want, your minimum pay, how many a day, and
whether it should submit on its own.

## What it will never do
Type a password or verification code, create an account, solve a CAPTCHA, enter your SSN, date of birth, driver's license or bank details,
or claim something on your resume you haven't confirmed. Those stay with you, so some applications will wait for you at the computer.

## Things to know
- **Applications go out in your name.** Start with "review each first" until you trust it.
- Automated tools can break some job sites' rules, and LinkedIn can restrict accounts. It searches lightly, but the choice and risk are yours.
- Running while you're away means letting Claude act without asking for permission each time. Setup explains this before you choose.
- Not affiliated with Anthropic, LinkedIn, Indeed or any job site.

## Updating
Tell Claude "update the kit". Your `me/` folder isn't touched.
